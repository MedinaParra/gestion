Royal Spin 3.0 includes the following third-party open-source runtimes during the Android build:

PixiJS 8.19.0

License: MIT
Source: https://github.com/pixijs/pixijs

PixiJS Filters 6.1.5
Copyright (c) PixiJS community contributors
License: MIT
Source: https://github.com/pixijs/filters

The runtime files are pinned to exact versions and packaged locally in the APK. Royal Spin does not load them from the network while the application is running.
